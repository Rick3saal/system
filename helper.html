BO Quick Helper

How to use: in the BO list, click the masked number/email until the full value appears and is copied. Come back here, paste it below, then press the button you need. Nothing is installed and nothing is sent anywhere.

Edit email templates

One per line: Name | Subject | Body. Use \n for a new line. {client} is replaced by the name above.

You can also turn on Gmail's own Templates in Gmail → Settings → See all settings → Advanced → Templates. That works without any approval from IT.
